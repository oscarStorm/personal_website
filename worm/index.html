Procedural Animation

This worm uses inverse kinematics to animate a chain of connected joints. The head follows a moving target, while the body follows using the forward-reaching phase of FABRIK (Forward And Backward Reaching Inverse Kinematics).

Each joint maintains a fixed distance from the one ahead, creating smooth, coordinated movement.

The target automatically steers away from screen boundaries, causing the chain to continuously adjust its shape and direction. The resulting motion emerges from simple geometric constraints rather than predefined animation frames.
